## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Keep every pet's health history in one place, shared instantly with everyone in
your household.

**Track what matters**
- Vaccines, with due dates so nothing sneaks up on you
- Medications, on a custom schedule that matches how your vet actually prescribed it
- Vet visits and follow-ups
- Weight over time, with a simple trend chart
- Expenses, so you know what you're really spending
- Documents and lab results, organized per pet

**Built for the whole household**
Everyone in your home sees the same up-to-date record for every pet — no more
"did anyone give her the pill today?" texts. Invite household members with a
simple code.

**Never miss a dose**
Reminders for upcoming vaccines, medication doses, and vet follow-ups, right on
your phone.

**Going away? Bring in a sitter.**
Grant a pet sitter temporary, read-only access to exactly the pets they're
watching — nothing else, and it expires automatically.

**One-page vet passport**
Generate a clean, one-page summary of a pet's vaccine history to bring to a new
vet or a boarding facility.

**Your data stays yours**
No ads. No selling your pet's health data. See the full privacy policy for
details on what's collected and why.
