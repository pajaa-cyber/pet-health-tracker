What this app is

Pet Health Tracker helps a household track their pets' vaccines, medications, vet visits, weight, expenses, and documents, shared in real time between household members you invite.

What we collect

  • Account info: the email address you sign up with (via Firebase Authentication). We never see or store your password directly.
  • Pet and household data you enter: pet profiles and photos, vaccines, medications, vet visits, weight logs, expenses, documents, and household member/invite information. Photos are stored as part of this data, not in a separate media service.
  • Sitter access: if you invite a pet sitter, we store a time-limited grant recording which pets they can view and when that access expires.

We do not collect location data, device identifiers for advertising, or any data beyond what you and your household members enter into the app.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no ad-tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service — the app doesn't include one.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Where your data is stored

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), Google's cloud infrastructure. We do not run our own servers.

Who can see your data

Only members of your household (and any sitter you've explicitly and temporarily granted access to) can see your pets' data. Household membership is invite-only.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept as long as your account and household exist. There is currently no self-service account deletion in the app; contact us below to request deletion of your account and associated data.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended to knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly.
